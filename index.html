Get help

Prepfolk is provided by Lahbib Semlali. For help or feedback, email lahbibdev@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Prepfolk version/build, what you tapped and what happened. A screenshot can help; remove personal information first. Never send passwords, verification codes, private keys or payment-card details.

Packs, purchases and restore

Four packs are included. Additional individual packs and the fixed four-pack Lunch Collection are offered as one-time purchases where available. The collection does not promise future packs. Apple shows the actual localized price before confirmation.

In Prepfolk, open Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore recovers eligible pack, collection or legacy Pro access; it does not restore local meal plans or progress. If an owned pack still looks unavailable, contact support with the app version and what you see. Do not send your Apple password or payment-card details.

Existing subscriptions and refunds

The new app does not offer new subscriptions. An existing monthly or yearly Prepfolk Pro subscription may still renew unless you cancel through Apple. Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prepfolk to manage renewal. Deleting the app or resetting its data does not cancel a subscription.

Apple’s cancellation instructions and refund request page explain the available options. Refund eligibility and outcomes are handled by Apple under applicable rules. A permanent pack purchase is separate from legacy subscription expiry.

Saved plans and progress

Kitchen preferences, Prep Plans, saved preps, shopping checkmarks, preparation progress and measured cooked weights are stored on your device. Prepfolk does not provide an account or cross-device plan sync. Avoid resetting or uninstalling the app while investigating missing progress.

Settings → Reset device data removes local plans, history, preferences and saved packs and requests local diagnostics erasure. It does not cancel legacy Apple subscriptions or erase purchase records.

Optional local diagnostics

Settings → Local usage counts is off by default. If you enable it, only bounded action counts are kept locally; no food details or automatic uploads are added. View local diagnostics lets you inspect a report. Share local diagnostics opens the system share sheet only when you choose it; you select a destination and may cancel.

You do not have to share a report to ask for help. Turning counts off or choosing Erase local diagnostics removes the stored counts and disables collection. If erasure fails, retry in Settings. Erasing local data cannot remove reports already shared elsewhere.

Cooking and portion questions

Tell us the pack and meal name, the step in question, the raw ingredient quantity and any cooked weight you measured. Share only the details needed for your question. Raw and cooked weights are different. Portions organize your meals; they do not calculate a personal nutrition prescription.

Check ingredients and packaging for allergens. Follow each component’s cooling, storage, freezing, thawing and reheating instructions. If instructions appear incorrect, stop and contact us. USDA food-storage guidance provides general information.
