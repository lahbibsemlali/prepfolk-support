Get help

Prepfolk is provided by Lahbib Semlali. For help or feedback, email lahbibdev@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Prepfolk version/build, what you tapped and what happened. A screenshot can help; remove personal information first. Never send passwords, verification codes, private keys or payment-card details.

Purchases and subscriptions

In Prepfolk, open Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore purchases restores eligible Pro access; it does not restore meal plans or progress.

To manage renewal, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prepfolk. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple’s cancellation instructions and refund request page explain the available options.

Saved plans and progress

Kitchen preferences, saved weeks, shopping checkmarks, preparation progress and measured cooked weights are stored on your device. Prepfolk does not provide an account or cross-device plan sync. Avoid resetting or uninstalling the app while investigating missing progress.

Settings → Reset device data removes local plans, history, preferences and saved packs. It does not cancel your Apple subscription.

Cooking and portion questions

Tell us the pack and meal name, the step in question, the raw ingredient quantity and any cooked weight you measured. Raw and cooked weights are different. Portions organize your meals; they do not calculate a personal nutrition prescription.

Check ingredients and packaging for allergens. Follow each component’s cooling, storage, freezing, thawing and reheating instructions. If instructions appear incorrect, stop and contact us. USDA food-storage guidance provides general information.

Privacy and terms

Read the Prepfolk privacy policy and Terms of use. For privacy requests, email the same support address and describe your request.
