How Prepfolk handles information.

Information stored on your device

Prepfolk stores your kitchen preferences, meal plans, saved weeks, shopping checkmarks, preparation progress and measured cooked weights on your device. App code does not upload these records to a Prepfolk server. No Prepfolk account is required.

The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, contacts, location or HealthKit. Device backups and their retention depend on your operating system and account settings.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information, an automatically generated anonymous app identifier, and technical information such as app, device and operating-system details. This is used to validate purchases, provide Pro access, prevent fraud and understand subscription activity.

Prepfolk does not provide your name, email, kitchen preferences or meal plans to RevenueCat as customer attributes. We do not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat’s subscription analytics are separate from the local meal-planning records.

See Apple’s privacy information and RevenueCat’s privacy policy for their processing and retention practices.

Support messages

If you email lahbibdev@gmail.com, your email address and anything you choose to send are used to respond to and investigate your request. This may include your name, device details, screenshots or a description of a problem. Support email uses Gmail; Google’s privacy policy describes that service.

Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Ask us to remove support correspondence you no longer want retained; some records may need to be kept to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the service. Read GitHub’s privacy statement.

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms or third-party font requests. Visiting an external link is subject to that provider’s privacy practices.

Advertising and sharing

Prepfolk contains no advertising SDK or cross-company advertising tracking. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information is handled by the service providers described above for app functionality, subscription operations, support and website delivery.

Retention and deletion

Local app records remain until removed through app actions, device-data reset or operating-system behavior. Settings → Reset device data removes local plans, progress, history, preferences and saved packs. Uninstalling may also remove local data.

Resetting or uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions, erase Apple or RevenueCat transaction records, delete operating-system backups, or delete email you have sent to support. Those records are handled separately under applicable provider practices and legal obligations.

Privacy requests and changes

Contact lahbibdev@gmail.com for questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion of information you have shared with us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to object to or restrict processing, or complain to your local privacy authority. We may need information to verify a request without asking for account passwords.

The providers listed above may process information in countries other than your own. Their linked policies explain their international processing practices. Material changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
