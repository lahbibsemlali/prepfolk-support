Information stored on your device

Prepfolk stores your kitchen preferences, Prep Plans, saved preps, shopping checkmarks, preparation progress and measured cooked weights on your device. App code does not upload these records to a Prepfolk server. No Prepfolk account is required.

The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, contacts, location or HealthKit. Device backups and their retention depend on your operating system and account settings.

Optional local usage counts

Local usage counts are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Prepfolk stores bounded counts of allowlisted actions, such as starting a Prep Plan or marking a first lunch eaten, on your device. Local technical entries help avoid counting the same prep milestone twice; these entries are not included in reports. No meal names, ingredients, dietary preferences, grocery contents, food measurements, receipts or RevenueCat customer information are recorded in these counts.

Nothing is uploaded automatically. You can view the report before choosing Share local diagnostics, which opens the system share sheet. You decide whether to send it and which destination to use; the destination has its own information-handling practices. The report reveals coarse app activity, so review it before sharing.

Turning local counts off or choosing Erase local diagnostics removes stored counts and disables collection. Device-data reset also requests diagnostics erasure. If storage prevents erasure, Settings reports the problem and offers retry. These actions cannot remove reports already shared or copies held in operating-system or browser backups. Counts are ordinary local application storage, not an encrypted vault.

Purchases and legacy subscriptions

Apple processes in-app purchase payments. Prepfolk offers permanent non-consumable pack purchases and a fixed Lunch Collection where available. Existing monthly or yearly Prepfolk Pro subscriptions remain supported while valid; they may continue renewing unless cancelled through Apple. No new subscription purchase is offered by the new app.

RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information, an automatically generated RevenueCat App User ID, and technical app, store, device and operating-system information used for purchase operations. This supports purchase validation, access and restore, fraud prevention and purchase-activity analytics. This processing is separate from optional local usage counts and is not disabled by turning those counts off.

Prepfolk does not provide your name, email, kitchen preferences or meal plans to RevenueCat as customer attributes. We do not receive your payment-card details.

See Apple’s privacy information and RevenueCat’s privacy policy for their processing and retention practices.

Support messages

If you email lahbibdev@gmail.com, your email address and anything you choose to send are used to respond to and investigate your request. This may include your name, device details, screenshots, a voluntarily shared local diagnostics report or a description of a problem. Support email uses Gmail; Google’s privacy policy describes that service.

Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Ask us to remove support correspondence you no longer want retained; some records may need to be kept to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the service. Read GitHub’s privacy statement.

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms or third-party font requests. Visiting an external link is subject to that provider’s privacy practices.

Advertising and sharing

Prepfolk contains no advertising SDK or cross-company advertising tracking. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information is handled by the service providers described above for app functionality, purchase operations, support and website delivery. Prepfolk does not upload local diagnostics. The app offers report sharing only through your explicit choice to open the system share sheet and select a destination.

Retention and deletion

Local app records remain until removed through app actions, device-data reset or operating-system behavior. Settings → Reset device data removes local plans, progress, history, preferences and saved packs and requests local diagnostics erasure. Uninstalling may also remove local data.

Resetting or uninstalling does not cancel legacy subscriptions, erase Apple or RevenueCat transaction records, delete operating-system backups, or delete reports and email you previously shared. Those records are handled separately under applicable provider practices and legal obligations. Restoring purchases restores eligible access, not local plans or progress.

Privacy requests and changes

Contact lahbibdev@gmail.com for questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion of information you have shared with us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to object to or restrict processing, or complain to your local privacy authority. We may need information to verify a request without asking for account passwords.

The providers listed above may process information in countries other than your own. Their linked policies explain their international processing practices. Material changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
