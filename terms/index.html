Included packs and permanent purchases

Prepfolk helps organize three one-person lunches, shared ingredients, shopping and preparation. Four reusable packs are included: Chicken, three ways; Tofu, three ways; Paprika chicken & couscous; and Sesame tofu noodles.

Where offered, additional packs are one-time non-consumable purchases. An individual purchase unlocks its named pack. The Lunch Collection unlocks exactly Turkey & black bean chilli, Lemon salmon & dill potatoes, Red lentil & spinach dhal, and Chickpea vegetable quinoa. It does not include future packs or a promise of future releases.

Non-consumable access does not expire over time like a subscription. If Apple refunds or revokes a purchase, eligibility for new paid Prep Plans can change. Existing saved plans and readable history remain available. Apple shows the actual localized price and purchase confirmation; do not purchase based on features or content that are not currently available.

Legacy subscription billing and renewal

The new app does not offer new monthly or yearly subscriptions. Existing Prepfolk Pro subscriptions remain supported while valid. They may continue renewing unless cancelled through Apple; changing the app’s offer, deleting Prepfolk or resetting its data does not cancel them.

Manage or cancel through iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prepfolk. An existing subscription cancelled for renewal can retain access through its remaining paid period as reported by Apple. A permanent pack purchase is separate from legacy subscription expiry.

See Apple’s cancellation instructions. Refund requests are handled through Apple, subject to applicable rules. No refund outcome is guaranteed by this page.

Restore purchases and device data

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase to recover eligible pack, collection or legacy subscription access. Restoring purchases does not restore meal plans or progress.

Plans and progress are stored locally. There is no Prepfolk account or cross-device meal-plan sync. Resetting device data, uninstalling, or losing the device can remove saved work. Prepfolk is not a backup service. Local reset does not erase Apple or RevenueCat transaction records.

Cooking, ingredients and portions

Prepfolk is a meal-planning and cooking-organization tool, not individualized medical or nutrition advice. Raw ingredients and cooked yields differ; use the measured cooked weights requested by the app. Portions are organizational guidance rather than a personal protein prescription. Cooking times are estimates. Food images are illustrative, not proof of kitchen testing.

Review ingredient packaging, substitutions and allergen information yourself. Preference filters are not an allergen-free guarantee. Follow appropriate cooking, cooling, storage, freezing, thawing and reheating guidance for the ingredients you use. Draft content is identified in the app and is not represented as kitchen-tested or nutrition-approved. Contact support about unclear or incorrect instructions.
