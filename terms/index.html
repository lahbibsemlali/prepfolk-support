Free and Pro access

Prepfolk helps organize lunches, shared ingredients, shopping and batch preparation. The free starter is reusable. Pro provides access to additional paid packs while the subscription is active. Your current saved week and readable history remain available after Pro expires.

Content availability and subscription access are shown in the app. Do not purchase based on features or content that are not currently available.

Subscription billing and renewal

The available plans renew monthly or yearly. Apple displays your localized price, billing period and purchase confirmation before you subscribe. Current plans do not include a free trial.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account after purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel automatic renewal before the renewal date. Manage or cancel through iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Prepfolk. Deleting Prepfolk or resetting its data does not cancel the subscription.

See Apple’s cancellation instructions. Refund requests are handled through Apple, subject to applicable rules. Restore purchases in Prepfolk using the Apple Account that purchased.

Device data and saved work

Plans and progress are stored locally. There is no Prepfolk account or cross-device meal-plan sync. Resetting device data, uninstalling, or losing the device can remove saved work. Prepfolk is not a backup service. Restoring purchases does not restore local plans.

Cooking, ingredients and portions

Prepfolk is a meal-planning and cooking-organization tool, not individualized medical or nutrition advice. Raw ingredients and cooked yields differ; use the measured cooked weights requested by the app. Portions are organizational guidance rather than a personal protein prescription.

Review ingredient packaging, substitutions and allergen information yourself. Preference filters are not an allergen-free guarantee. Follow appropriate cooking, cooling, storage, freezing, thawing and reheating guidance for the ingredients you use. Contact support about unclear or incorrect instructions.
